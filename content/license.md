## Clear Ownership. Zero Hostage-Taking.

Most agencies either lock you into a proprietary black box or bury IP ownership in fine print. At **Musa Labs**, our licensing and ownership terms are designed to be as clear as our workflows.

---

## 1. Client Workflow & Code Ownership

Depending on how you choose to run your automations, here is how ownership and licensing work:

### Option A: Self-Hosted ("We Build It, You Run It")
- Upon final payment of the agreed project fee, **you own 100% of the custom workflow code, configuration, and Flow Engineering documentation** delivered for your business.
- We grant you a perpetual, irrevocable, royalty-free license to run, modify, and extend any reusable Musa Labs internal libraries or templates included in your build for your own business operations.

### Option B: Fully Managed & Hosted by Musa Labs
- While Musa Labs hosts, monitors, and maintains your workflows under an active monthly support agreement, **you retain 100% ownership of all your business data, documents, prompts, and documented process flows**.
- **No Lock-In / Buyout Right**: You are never trapped. At any point during a managed engagement in good standing, you may elect to transition your workflows to your own Cloudflare account in accordance with your service agreement.

---

## 2. Confidentiality & Data Ownership

- **Your Data is Yours**: All customer records, invoices, PDFs, SOPs, and credentials processed through your workflows remain the exclusive property of your business.
- **Strict Confidentiality**: Musa Labs treats your internal business processes, pricing rules, API credentials, and company documents as strictly confidential.

---

## 3. Third-Party Services & AI Responsibility

- **External Platforms**: Your automations may connect to third-party software you use (such as HubSpot, Salesforce, QuickBooks, Slack, Stripe, OpenAI, or Anthropic). Your use of those underlying platforms remains governed by their respective terms of service.
- **Human-in-the-Loop Controls**: Where AI models are used to draft communications, extract data, or assist with decisions, we engineer human-in-the-loop approval steps as agreed in your scope of work. Final business, financial, and compliance decisions remain under your team's control.
