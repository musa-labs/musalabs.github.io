## Stop Answering the Same Questions Ten Times a Day

Every company has valuable knowledge trapped inside folders of PDFs, standard operating procedures (SOPs), employee handbooks, old proposals, and email threads. When someone on your team needs an answer, they either waste 20 minutes digging through folders—or they interrupt you.

We build **private Company Knowledge AI assistants** trained exclusively on your business's documents, accessible right from Slack, Microsoft Teams, or a simple web portal.

### How Teams Use Company Knowledge AI

- **Internal Team Copilot**: Staff can ask *"What's our policy on rush orders?"* or *"How do we configure Model X for a commercial client?"* and get the exact step-by-step instructions and source link in under two seconds.
- **Customer Support Draft Assistant**: Automatically suggest accurate, policy-backed replies to incoming customer questions based on your actual manuals and warranty docs—never made-up guesses.
- **Proposal & Contract Lookup**: Quickly search across years of past agreements, scopes of work, and pricing sheets without opening fifty different files.

### Private, Fast, and Hallucination-Free

Uploading sensitive company files into a random public AI tool is a security risk, and generic chatbots often make things up when they don't know the answer.

We build your knowledge assistant on **Cloudflare Vectorize, R2, and Workers AI**:
1. **Strict Grounding (No Guessing)**: The assistant is instructed to answer *only* using verified facts found in your uploaded documents, and it always attaches a link to the source file so you can verify it in one click.
2. **Automatic Sync**: Whenever you add or update a document in your storage bucket, an automated background worker indexes the changes immediately.
3. **Complete Data Ownership**: All files and search indexes stay inside your own Cloudflare account—giving you enterprise-grade privacy without enterprise software licensing fees.
